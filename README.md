# Peng-Zong
**Faculty Member, Fuyang Normal University, China**

I am an astrophysicist studying stellar pulsation and asteroseismology, with a particular interest in RR Lyrae stars. My research combines time-series photometry from Kepler/K2 and TESS with spectroscopic data from LAMOST and measurements from Gaia to investigate stellar pulsation, the Blazhko effect, and stellar properties and evolution.

## Research Interests

- RR Lyrae stars and stellar pulsation
- Asteroseismology and additional pulsation modes
- The Blazhko effect
- Time-domain astronomy and time-series data analysis
- Stellar evolution and distance measurements

## Education

- Ph.D. in Astrophysics, Beijing Normal University, 2024
- M.Sc. in Astrophysics, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, 2019
